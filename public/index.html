From account setup to verified payment in four steps.

A focused workflow keeps merchants in control while customers get a fast, mobile-ready UPI checkout.

STEP 01

Connect an account

Add your business name, mobile number and merchant UPI details. Link your banking notification feed in seconds.

STEP 02

Create an order or payment link

Use the dashboard or developer REST API with amount, customer details and callback data.

STEP 03

Customer completes UPI payment

Share the hosted checkout with dynamic QR, deep UPI intent links for GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, and reference submission.

STEP 04

Verify and automate

The matching engine verifies the transaction in ~1 second, credits your bank, and dispatches the configured webhook.

A clean API, without the integration maze.

    "amount": 1499.00,
