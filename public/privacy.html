2. Categories of Digital Personal Data Collected

Under the strict doctrine of Data Minimization (Section 6(1) of the DPDP Act 2023), the Platform collects and processes solely the minimum data fields necessary to provide identity authentication, dynamic order generation, and payment matching:

2.1. Identity & Account Information: When authenticating via Google OAuth (Firebase Authentication), we receive: (a) Merchant primary email address, (b) Merchant full name or display pseudonym, and (c) Google user profile image URL. We do NOT receive or store your Google account password.

2.2. Financial Reconciliation Telemetry: For the singular purpose of matching and verifying UPI transactions via automated email monitoring, the engine processes:

  • The 12-digit Unique Transaction Reference (UTR / UPI Ref Number).
  • The transaction timestamp recorded by the issuing bank.
  • The exact payment amount (including fractional paise offsets).
  • The sender's name or masked UPI ID as supplied within the bank notification email.

2.3.
